Yes. You can download and access your digital study materials on compatible devices that support the respective file formats.

11 What should I do if I don't receive my sample or purchased materials?
First, check your Spam, Promotions and Updates folders. If you still cannot find the email, contact our support team with your registered email address and payment details, if applicable.

Yes. You can purchase multiple courses separately. Each purchase will provide access to its respective course materials.
